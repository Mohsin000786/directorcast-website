Production-Grade Capabilities

Built from the ground up for live streamers, event directors, podcast hosts, and mobile journalists.

01

Instant QR Pairing

No manual IP addresses or complex routing. Secondary camera nodes join the master studio session instantly by scanning an on-screen QR code over local Wi-Fi.

02

True Voice AI Auto-Director

Hands-free multi-cam switching. Built-in audio direction algorithms analyze speaker voice location and automatically trigger camera cuts to the active speaker.

03

4K ISO Multi-Track Recording

Record isolated, uncompressed 4K video streams locally on every paired device while simultaneously outputting the master live stream for post-production editing.

04

Custom RTMP Streaming

Broadcast directly to YouTube Live, Twitch, Facebook Live, or custom private RTMP/RTMPS server destinations with live bitrate control.

05

Zero-Latency Local Core

Direct socket network protocol streams local camera feeds with ultra-low latency (`NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES`), ensuring sync accuracy across all angles.

06

Tally Light System

Hardware-style visual tally indicators on camera node screens show operators instantly when their feed is Live (Red) or on Preview (Blue).

How It Works

1

Launch Master Studio

Open DirectorCast on your main device to start a production session and generate a secure local QR pairing key.

2

Connect Camera Nodes

Scan the master QR code using secondary Android phones. Feeds instantly pop up in your studio switcher console.

3

Switch & Stream Live

Manually tap angles, enable True Voice AI auto-switching, record multi-track ISO files, and go live to your chosen RTMP platform.
